Posted from the project account. **English, no name on either side, no figure in
public** — the rate is discussed in DMs, because a public number becomes the
anchor for everyone who reads it afterwards.

Looking for **one illustrator** for a 4,000-piece collection on Solana.

Black dragons. Painted volume, not flat. Three-quarter bust, muted solid field,
internal fire, chains and streetwear. Warm rather than menacing.

The brief is unusual in one way and it is the whole job: **the piece has to read
in a 48 px circle**. Silhouette plus one bright seam. Everything else is detail
for people who zoom in.

Eight layers, a fixed variant list, exact counts. Three milestones, paid at each,
starting with three finished pieces so we both find out early whether this works.

I work pseudonymously and I do not need your name either. Portfolio over
introductions — reply with work, and DMs are open.
