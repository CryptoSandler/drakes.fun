Drakes

4,000 pieces. None of them can be bought at issuance. One is issued every hour, by the protocol, to a holder of $DRAKES, in proportion to what they hold.

Every trade of the token pays a fee into a hoard held in $PUMP. A piece can be burned to take its share of that hoard, and the place it leaves is never refilled.

Once the first piece is issued, the collection takes

166 days 16 hours

— and that is a floor, not a date. Any hour that fails to settle pushes it out by an hour. Nothing is scheduled yet.

Rarity is fixed and published before the first piece exists, and it is cosmetic: the rarest piece redeems for exactly the same share as the plainest one.

This site will show the hoard, the schedule and the arithmetic of every issuance, so a stranger can recompute all of it from the chain.
